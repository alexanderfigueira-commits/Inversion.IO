Meditation

Five minutes of calm, wherever you are.

Ready?

Breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 2, breathe out for 6.

  1. 1. Sit comfortablyBack straight but relaxed, shoulders loose, hands resting in your lap.
  2. 2. Follow your breathDon't try to control it. Just notice the air coming in and going out.
  3. 3. Come back when you driftThoughts will show up. Acknowledge them and return to your breath, without judging yourself.
  4. 4. Finish slowlyWiggle your fingers, open your eyes and take this calm into the rest of your day.
